## Positioning and messaging

### HelioDesk

- **Current claim:** “The AI copilot for every support team. Deflect more tickets with instant, accurate answers.”
- **Observable mismatch:** One of ten prospects repeats “AI support copilot.” Six say “AI support automation,” and three use governance-oriented language.
- **Underused attributes:** Policy grounding, human approval, escalation controls, and audit logging are more relevant to current buyer language than setup speed alone.
- **Claim constraint:** The audit log and cost-per-resolution reporting are beta. The 99.3% policy-citation result is internally audited and not independently validated.

### ResolveGrid

- **Positioning angle:** Governance-first control layer for autonomous resolution.
- **Strength:** Its language matches cross-functional risk and operations concerns appearing in recent HelioDesk losses.
- **Evidence:** Four Q2 losses named ResolveGrid. In three, the account executive had not identified the company during qualification.
- **Unknown:** The fixture does not show ResolveGrid’s product performance, pricing, customer retention, or implementation burden.

### Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI

- **Positioning angles:** Resolution-rate performance and installed-suite automation.
- **Strengths:** Intercom owns a direct outcome frame. Zendesk can sell workflow breadth inside an established suite.
- **Unknown:** The fixture supplies no win-loss counts, proof, pricing, or buyer objections for either.

## Strengths

- Generally available policy grounding, human approval, and escalation controls.
- Deployment speed already recognized by leaders and reference customers.
- A product direction that matches buyer movement toward observable control.

## Weaknesses

- Public positioning uses only two of the twelve frequent buyer-language terms.
- Leadership cannot state one shared reason customers choose HelioDesk.
- Competitive qualification missed ResolveGrid in three of four named Q2 losses.
- Governance proof is not mature enough for broad compliance or safety claims.

## Opportunities and threats

- **Opportunity:** Own the combination of controlled resolution and deployment continuity instead of imitating ResolveGrid’s category.
- **Opportunity:** Add risk and operations stakeholders to qualification and proof packaging.
- **Threat:** ResolveGrid can keep defining the governance problem before HelioDesk appears in the evaluation.
- **Threat:** Intercom and Zendesk can compress HelioDesk between outcome ownership and suite breadth.

## Positioning recommendation

**Replace the external lead frame.** Test a controlled, defensible resolution position for the existing mid-market segment and its expanding buying group. Preserve ticket deflection and deployment speed as supporting value. Do not claim a new category until buyer tests show that prospects understand and prefer the proposed frame.

Store raw captures before synthesis and rerun the comparison monthly during the 90-day reset.

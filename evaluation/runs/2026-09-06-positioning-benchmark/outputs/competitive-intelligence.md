# HelioDesk Competitive Positioning Update

**As of:** 2026-08-15
**Audience:** CEO, CRO, CPO, and VP Marketing
**Decision:** Replace, refresh, or preserve the January 2023 position

## What moved

ResolveGrid entered in November 2024 with a governance-first story. AI governance began entering support procurement in September 2024, but HelioDesk did not respond in positioning or packaging. Four Q2 losses named ResolveGrid, and three account executives failed to identify it during qualification.

The broader category shifted from access and setup speed toward controlled autonomous resolution. Intercom frames around resolution rate. Zendesk frames around suite automation. ResolveGrid frames around proof, approval, and auditability. HelioDesk still leads with an “AI copilot” and ticket deflection.

## Threat map

| Competitor | Threat type | Where it wins | HelioDesk response |
|---|---|---|---|
| ResolveGrid | Direct threat | Governance language, audit trail, cross-functional buyer | Lead with the buyer’s need for defensible resolution, not “control plane”; qualify risk stakeholders early. |
| Intercom Fin | Direct or displacement risk | Resolution-rate outcome and integrated data | Pair control with measurable resolution quality and cost, without inventing proof. |
| Zendesk AI | Installed-suite threat | Workflow breadth and incumbent distribution | Emphasize deployable control across existing support operations. |
| Build or tolerate risk | Indirect alternatives | Avoids vendor change or adds internal review | Show where grounding, approval, escalation, and override reduce operating burden. |

## Red flags already triggered

- Prospects know the active threat before the sales team identifies it.
- The intended category is repeated by only one of ten prospects.
- The market description moved from “AI Support Innovator” to “Established Copilot Vendor.”
- Win rate fell from 34% to 22% while sales cycles and discounting rose.
- Leadership has no shared answer for why the best-fit customer chooses HelioDesk.

## Decision

**Replace the external lead frame.** Do not preserve “AI copilot” as the category. Do not copy ResolveGrid’s “resolution governance control plane.” Test a position around controlled, defensible resolution for mid-market support leaders and the operations and risk stakeholders who shape the purchase.

Keep ticket deflection and deployment speed as supporting proof. They remain valuable to the installed base and reduce the cost of changing the frame.

## Claims to start, qualify, and stop

### Start

- Policy-grounded answers with human approval, escalation, and override.
- Controlled resolution that can be inspected and defended.
- Deployment continuity for teams that already value speed and deflection.

### Qualify

- The 99.3% policy-citation result is an internal audited sample, not independent validation.
- The 41% median escalation reduction comes from four selected beta customers.
- Audit logging and cost-per-resolution reporting are beta.

### Stop

- “AI copilot” as the lead category.
- Unqualified compliance, safety, or accuracy guarantees.
- Treating Support as the only relevant stakeholder during qualification.

## Positioning hypothesis

> HelioDesk gives mid-market support teams controlled AI resolution they can inspect and defend, without giving up deployment speed.

This is a test hypothesis, not approved copy.

## Kill criteria

Return to a refresh-only path if the replacement frame fails to improve category comprehension or creates installed-base confusion. Narrow the claim if two live opportunities accept the frame but stop on missing audit, certification, or beta evidence.

## Intelligence cadence

- **Weekly:** Live opportunity criteria, named competitors, buying-group participation, and proof objections.
- **Monthly:** Competitor messaging, product proof, pricing signals, and loss themes.
- **Triggered:** Review within one week of a competitor control launch, pricing change, or named enterprise win.
- **Day 60:** Commit, narrow, or revert the position using the preregistered criteria.

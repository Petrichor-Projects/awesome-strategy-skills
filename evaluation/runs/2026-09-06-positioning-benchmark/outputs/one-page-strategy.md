# One Page Strategy: HelioDesk Positioning Reset

**Scope**: Company positioning and go-to-market system
**Horizon**: September to November 2026

## The Facts

Customers now ask HelioDesk to make AI-supported resolutions defensible through grounded answers, policy control, audit trails, escalation safety, and human override. HelioDesk still leads with a January 2023 “AI copilot” and ticket-deflection position, even though only one of ten prospects uses that category and ten of the twelve most frequent buyer terms are absent from the homepage. Qualified-opportunity win rate fell from 34% to 22%, the median sales cycle rose from 46 to 64 days, average discounting doubled to 28%, and net revenue retention fell to 96%. ResolveGrid has won four recent deals with governance language, yet HelioDesk already ships policy grounding and approval controls and is testing an audit log and cost-per-resolution reporting. The installed base still values ticket deflection, and the current proof does not support unqualified compliance, safety, or accuracy claims.

## The Problem

Customer challenge: during the next 90 days, HelioDesk must make its control advantage visible without copying ResolveGrid or disowning the installed base’s deflection value.

## The Idea

We believe that framing HelioDesk as controlled, defensible resolution will restore relevance if deployment speed becomes proof and every governance claim stays qualified.

## The Solution

- Replace “AI copilot” as the lead frame with a testable controlled-resolution hypothesis for mid-market support, operations, and risk buying groups.
- Preserve ticket deflection and deployment speed as continuity proof for current customers, not as the category claim.
- Package generally available policy grounding, approval, escalation, and human override separately from beta audit and reporting evidence.
- Test the new frame in ten active opportunities and five customer conversations against the current position and a refresh-only alternative.
- Stop unqualified compliance, safety, and accuracy language; review win rate, cycle time, buying-group coverage, and claim rejection after 30, 60, and 90 days.

## Inversion check

- **Falsifiability:** Reject the new frame if buyers do not understand it more accurately than the current one, if governance does not affect evaluation criteria, or if it increases installed-base confusion.
- **Trade-off:** HelioDesk is not claiming a new governance category or leading with setup speed alone.
- **Credible alternative:** Refresh the current position with stronger control proof if buyer tests show that “AI support automation” remains the clearest frame and governance acts only as a deal qualifier.

# Mentioned Assets

Authors of included skills and collections may use these assets. They indicate editorial inclusion in the catalog, not certification, sponsorship, security review, or a specific evaluation score.

## Compact badge

The repository includes a branded [SVG badge](../assets/mentioned-in-awesome-strategy-skills-badge.svg):

```markdown
[![Mentioned in Awesome Strategy Skills](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Petrichor-Projects/awesome-strategy-skills/main/assets/mentioned-in-awesome-strategy-skills-badge.svg)](https://github.com/Petrichor-Projects/awesome-strategy-skills)
```

Use this smaller Shields version when a full image is too large:

```markdown
[![Listed in Awesome Strategy Skills](https://img.shields.io/badge/listed-Awesome%20Strategy%20Skills-0B5D5B)](https://github.com/Petrichor-Projects/awesome-strategy-skills)
```

## Share card

Use the [1200 × 630 share card](../assets/mentioned-in-awesome-strategy-skills.svg) for a post, release note, or repository social image. Do not crop out the “editorial inclusion” line or present it as a score or certification.

Maintainers may remove an entry under the [editorial policy](../EDITORIAL-POLICY.md). Badge use does not affect editorial decisions.

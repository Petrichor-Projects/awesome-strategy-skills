- 62 structured catalog records across nine outcome categories.
- 18 Petrichor Resilience Stack frameworks with maintainer conflict disclosed.
- A public evaluation rubric, reproducible fixture, pinned sources, complete outputs, and provisional evidence-level scores.

Do not claim independent validation, market leadership, adoption, or commercial results until evidence exists.

Most “awesome AI” lists optimize for volume.

## Community post

Most agent-skill lists tell you what exists. They do not tell you whether it can support a consequential decision.

The repository organizes strategy skills by outcome and reviews evidence discipline, workflow integrity, trade-offs, adversarial resilience, output utility, safety, and maintenance. ## Thirty-day sequence

| Window | Action | Primary measure |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Publish the repository, landing page, founder post, and company post. | Qualified repository views and site-to-GitHub clicks. |
| Days 1 to 3 | Notify included authors individually; lead with accuracy and correction rights. | Replies, corrections, shares, and badge adoption. |
| Days 4 to 7 | Share the open pilot in relevant practitioner communities. | Evaluation reads, substantive issues, reviewer interest. |
| Week 2 | Publish one rubric-dimension explainer using evidence from the pilot. | Saves, backlinks, and return visits. |
| Week 3 | Evaluate one community-nominated skill or comparison. | Nominations and independent participation. |
| Week 4 | Publish the first transparent metrics note and editorial changelog. | Referral quality and Resilience Stack click-through. |
